My Design Process

I like to understand first and create after. Those are the steps that guide my work from start to finish.

1.Empathize

Learn what the users need, feel, and struggle with by doing user interviews, surveys, and competitor analysis.

2.Define

Analyze my research to write a clear, user-centric problem statement and build user personas or journey maps.

3.Ideate

Brainstorm diverse solutions with the team, sketch rapid paper concepts, and map out structured user flows.

4.Prototype

Build an interactive, digital wireframes or mid-to-high fidelity prototypes to validate design and usability.

5.Test

Put prototypes in front of real users to watch how they interact, spot pain points, and gather direct feedback.

6.Iterate

Refine and re-test the product based on user findings before finalizing the design for developer handoff.

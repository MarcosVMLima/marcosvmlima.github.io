Project Name

Entouch Portal

Company

Entouch Controls

Briefing

Redesign the layout for the Entouch Controls Portal for management of HVAC and refrigeration systems.

Problem
  • The layout is outdated and needs lots of training to be able to work well with it;
  • Lots of clients use only a part of the system, not the whole.
Main Goal

Modernize the layout for the system and make it easier to find the information the client needs.

Research
User Interviews:
  • We need and easier system, it is too hard to find some things we need and takes lots of steps;
  • The look is really outdated, maybe do something more modern;
  • Some parts of the system need a better highlight, like the temperature widget.
Competitor Analysis:
  • Modern look and easier usability;
  • It is simple to access the important parts of the system.
Design Process
  • Interview users and stakeholders;
  • Reseach the competitors;
  • Review user flowchart;
  • Review pages needed in the system;
  • Design prototype.

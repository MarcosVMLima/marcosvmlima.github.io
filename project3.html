InnovareTI

Briefing

We need an easy way to alocate our employees when they need to come to the office, so they can book meeting rooms or working station respecting social distances.

Problem
  • It is hard to alocate everyone working on-site;
  • Need to keep social distancing.
Main Goal

Allow the employees to book stations when working on-site.

Research
User Interviews:
  • Need a safe spot to work when going on-site;
  • Have to know if there is everything I need to work;
  • An easy way to book a spot.
Competitor Analysis:
  • Don't show a map of the office to knowwhere is the spot you'll choose;
  • Easy to navigate, clean.
Design Process
  • Interview users and stakeholders;
  • Research the competitors;
  • Build user flowchart;
  • Design prototype.
